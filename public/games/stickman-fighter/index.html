How It Works

Are ya fighting, son? This is a fast-paced 1v1 stickman brawler. Win rounds, stack combos, and be the first to 2 round wins to take the match.

Let's Go, Son starts a solo match against smart AI. The rival adapts — it blocks, punishes whiffs, and strings combos together. Change difficulty anytime in Settings.

Pause mid-fight with Esc or the pause button. After a match, run it back or head home from the game-over screen.

Mobile / Touch

On phones and tablets, use the on-screen joystick (left) to move and jump. Attack buttons on the right cover Light Punch, Heavy Punch, Sweep Kick, and Block.

Toggle touch controls in Settings → Mobile Controls (Auto-detect, Always On, or Always Off). The game works best in landscape orientation.

Connect Phantom

Multiplayer and the leaderboard require a Phantom wallet. Your fighter name and round wins are saved to your wallet address.

Desktop: Install the Phantom browser extension, then tap Connect Phantom on the Play vs Friend screen.

Mobile: Tap Connect Phantom to open the Phantom app, approve the connection, and you'll return here automatically in the same browser.

First time? After connecting, pick a unique fighter name (2–14 characters). That name is tied to your wallet and shown on the leaderboard.

Reconnect anytime to load your profile, wins, and name. You must connect a wallet before creating or joining an online room.

Play vs Friend

From the main menu, choose Play vs Friend, connect your Phantom wallet, then either create a room or join one.

Create Room — generates a 6-character code. Share it with your opponent and wait for them to join. Copy the code with one tap.

Join Room — enter the code your friend sent and tap Join to connect.

Once both players are in, the fight starts automatically. First to 2 round wins takes the match. If your opponent disconnects, you'll return to the menu.

Round wins from online matches count toward your leaderboard total when your wallet is connected.

Round Wins Ranking

Open Leaderboard from the main menu to see the top fighters ranked by total round wins.

Each row shows rank, fighter name, and round-win count. Your row is highlighted when your wallet is connected.

Wins are earned in online multiplayer matches while connected with Phantom. Climb the board by winning rounds against real opponents, son.

Fight Smarter

• Win a round? Are ya winning, son? — yeah, you are.

• Lose a round? Shake it off and run it back.

• Block reduces incoming damage by 85%.

• Heavy Punch hits hardest — save it for when the rival whiffs.

• Sweep Kick hits low, good for mix-ups.

• Hard mode AI punishes your recovery frames.

• Adjust Master, Music, and SFX volume in Settings.
